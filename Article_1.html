From Hitting Bullseyes at KWBSC,
to Putting Down the Rifle: My Sudden Career Pause

Starting a new experience amidst the strict routines of an Islamic boarding school always leaves
a unique and lasting impression. For me, the journey into the world of target shooting began
back when I was still a student at a boarding school in Malang. It was there that my discipline
was tested—not only in spiritual matters, but also in mastering self-control through the scope of
a target rifle.

Amidst the tight daily schedule of the boarding school, I decided to join shooting as an
extracurricular activity. This decision led to an even bigger step. What started as a simple school
hobby quickly turned serious as my potential was recognized, eventually earning me a golden
opportunity to join a prestigious club: the Kota Wisata Batu Shooting Club (KWBSC) Malang.
Joining KWBSC marked a whole new chapter that deeply broadened my perspective on
discipline, focus, and composure.

Target shooting is never just about lining up a sight or pulling the trigger as fast as possible.
To me, it is an intense mental exercise—the art of controlling your breath, managing your
emotions, and maintaining absolute concentration within seconds. As a boarding student,
balancing religious studies, academic classes, and professional shooting practice added a
distinct rhythm to my days. Every training session with KWBSC in Malang was a moment I
eagerly anticipated to sharpen my accuracy, test my mental fortitude under pressure, and
connect with fellow target shooting enthusiasts.

I felt like my career was just about to take off, filled with dreams of achieving success on the
range. However, as time went on, life brought me to a crossroads. After graduating from the
boarding school, I had to move to Yogyakarta to pursue the next phase of my life and education.
This geographical transition, unfortunately, drastically shifted my daily activities and priorities.

Since settling in Jogja, my involvement in target shooting gradually wound down. The hustle of a
new academic life, adapting to a new environment, managing a different daily routine, and no
longer having the same regular access to training facilities as I had in Malang meant that my air
rifle and shooting gear ended up being stored away in the closet. From an active shooter at a
prestigious club, I slowly transitioned into an inactive member.

My shooting career, which felt so promising under the banner of KWBSC Malang, had to take
a sudden "career pause."

Even though my involvement in shooting is currently on pause, I have never regretted any of it.
The invaluable experience with Kota Wisata Batu Shooting Club remains a crucial and
inseparable part of my life story. The principles of unwavering focus, calmness under intense
pressure, and high self-discipline developed during my training days at KWBSC Malang continue
to serve me well. I carry all those lessons with me and apply them to my daily routine and new
challenges in Yogyakarta to this day.

I may have put down my rifle for now, but the spirit of discipline and focus it taught me will never be put down.
Who knows? maybe one day, I'll pull the trigger once again.

Before Leading a Home: Learning the Basics Behind the Stove

To me, the kitchen isn't foreign territory-it’s a new space to learn and explore.
My interestin cooking grew out of pure curiosity, backed by one simple intention :
I want to be a husband who can cook.

Knowing how to cook isn't just about basic survival; it’s about independence
and shared responsibility. I want a future where kitchen duties don't fall
entirely on one person's shoulders. When my future partner is exhausted or busy,
I want to step in and put a decent meal on the table.

The process of learning to cook has taught me patience, focus, and precision. From these initial
steps behind the stove, I know I'm building a practical skill that truly matters for the future.
